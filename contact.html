What to have ready when you call

None of this is required. A quick call is fine. But the more of it you have handy, the faster we can point you to the right tub or shower and give you a useful written quote.

  • Your city or ZIP code, so we know which building office covers your address.
  • Who will be bathing and how they move today: a cane, walker or wheelchair, trouble sitting down or standing up, or a caregiver who helps.
  • The tub area's size. Length, width and wall-to-wall depth of the current tub alcove. Our fit check shows where to measure.
  • The narrowest doorway on the way to the bathroom, including the bathroom door itself.
  • A few phone photos of the tub, the faucet wall and the bathroom from the doorway.
  • Your water heater's size, usually printed on its label, if you are considering a larger tub.
  • Any funding you are applying for, such as a VA HISA grant or a Colorado Medicaid home modification benefit, so the quote can match what that program needs. See paying for it.

Common questions before calling

Is the quote really free?

Yes. The quote is free, it is in writing, and there is no deadline to decide. Take the time you need and talk it over with family.

Will someone pressure me to buy?

No. Our job on the first call is to understand the bather and the bathroom and to answer questions. If a walk-in tub is not the right fit, we will say so, and a shower may be the better answer.

Do you only work in Denver?

We are based in Denver and cover the whole metro and the rest of Colorado. For homeowners farther away who have their own licensed contractor, Aging Safely Baths & More also offers supply-only shipping to all 50 states and Canada. See areas we serve.
