Permits and building offices differ across the metro

One practical difference between cities is who issues permits. Denver work goes through the city's Community Planning and Development department. Suburbs such as Aurora, Lakewood, Arvada, Westminster and Littleton run their own building divisions, and unincorporated communities such as Highlands Ranch go through the county instead. Rules and processing also vary, so whether a given walk-in tub or shower project needs a permit can depend on your exact address and on what changes, such as moving a drain or adding an electrical circuit for a heated seat or jets.

You do not need to sort this out yourself. Your installer should know which office covers your address and handle any permit the work requires. If you are comparing companies, our installer checklist lists the questions worth asking, including who pulls the permit.
