Walk-In Tubs for Lakewood Homes

Lakewood has a large share of the post-war ranch homes that define the west metro: one level, one hall bathroom, a standard tub in an alcove. That layout is often a very good fit for a walk-in tub, with a few things worth checking first.

The Lakewood ranch bathroom

A single-story ranch is about the friendliest house there is for aging in place. No stairs to the bedroom, short hallways, and a hall bath that is usually a straightforward rectangle. When we look at a Lakewood ranch bathroom, these are the questions that decide the plan:

  1. Is it the only full bathroom? In many ranches it is. If everyone in the house bathes there, we talk carefully about whether a walk-in tub or a walk-in shower serves the whole household better.
  2. What is under the floor? Ranches here typically sit over a basement or a crawlspace. A basement often makes reaching the drain from below easier; a tight crawlspace can make it harder.
  3. What shape is the subfloor in? A tub that has been in place for decades sometimes hides a slow leak. We plan for the possibility and agree how any repair would be handled before work starts.
  4. How wide is the bathroom door? Older interior doors can be narrow. It is one of the first measurements we ask for.
  5. Is the water heater up to it? Many older homes have the heater in the basement or a utility closet. We compare its capacity with the tub's fill volume.

When the only bathroom gets a walk-in tub

If a walk-in tub goes into the only full bath, plan for the rest of the household. Most walk-in tubs include a handheld shower, so others can still rinse while seated, and some surrounds allow a standard shower head above the tub. Standing showers in a walk-in tub work for some people, but the tub is designed around sitting. If a quick standing shower is important to others in the house, a low-threshold walk-in shower with a fold-down seat may be the better compromise. There is no wrong answer; it depends on who lives there.

Think about the next bathroom too. Some Lakewood ranches have a half bath or a basement bathroom. If a second shower exists or could be added, a walk-in tub in the main bath becomes an easier decision.

Lakewood resources for older adults

Lloyd G. Clements Community Center

1580 Yarrow St., Lakewood · City of Lakewood Active Adult Services

Lakewood's center for adults 55 and older offers classes, fitness, travel and transportation help. Its Reachout office provides referrals to community programs, including a benefits check-up, which is a practical way to learn what assistance you might qualify for. lakewood.org

Jefferson County Aging and Adult Services

Jefferson County Human Services

County help with Long-Term Care Medicaid, Old Age Pension and the Home Care Allowance. 303-271-1388. jeffco.us

DRCOG Area Agency on Aging

Jefferson County's Area Agency on Aging

Information and referral, options counseling, Medicare counseling and caregiver support at no cost. 303-480-6700. drcog.org

Brothers Redevelopment

Low- and fixed-income homeowners 60+ and people with disabilities

Jefferson County's resource listings include Brothers Redevelopment for home repair. Its home modification program lists shower conversions and grab bars. brothersredevelopment.org

Lakewood permits

Lakewood is its own city, so the City and County of Denver's permit rules do not apply here. Whether your project needs a permit depends on what changes, such as plumbing relocation or a new circuit for a jetted tub. Your installer handles permits where required.

Before installation day in a ranch

  • Clear the route from the front door to the hall bath, including the entry rug and any hall furniture.
  • If plumbing will be reached from the basement, clear space under the bathroom.
  • Know where the main water shutoff is; in many ranches it is in the basement or utility room.
  • Plan where everyone will shower that evening if this is the only bath, in case sealants need time to cure.

Most walk-in tub and roll-in shower installs are completed in one day. Aging Safely Baths & More has been doing them since 2012, and we are based in Denver, close to Lakewood.

If you are not sure where to begin, start with one question for the bather: do you want to keep taking baths? The answer narrows the choice more than anything else on this page.

Lakewood questions

My ranch has a crawlspace, not a basement. Is that a problem?

Usually not. It can make reaching the drain from below more work, which may affect the plan or price. Tell us when you call so we can account for it.

Can the Clements Center help me find financial assistance?

Its Reachout office provides referrals to community programs and a benefits check-up. It does not pay for remodeling itself, but it can point you to programs that might help.

Will a walk-in tub fit my 1950s alcove?

Many walk-in tubs are made to fit standard alcoves, but older homes vary. Measure the alcove length at two heights and send the smaller number.
