City of Littleton, or a Littleton address?

Many homes with a Littleton mailing address are outside the City of Littleton, in unincorporated Arapahoe, Jefferson or Douglas County, or in neighboring cities. That matters for three things: which building department handles permits, which county human services office you use, and whether city-only programs such as Littleton's Omnibus ride service are available to you. For aging services, DRCOG serves all three counties, so 303-480-6700 works for any Littleton address.

Getting to appointments: Littleton's Omnibus

A bathroom change often comes at the same time as more medical appointments: a new diagnosis, physical therapy after a surgery, follow-ups. The City of Littleton runs Omnibus, an appointment-based, door-to-door ride service for city residents who are 60 or older or who have a disability, including a temporary one. The city has contracted operations to Via Mobility Services, and riders register and book by phone. Hours and destinations have changed over the years, so check the city's Omnibus page for current details before planning around it.

Why mention transportation on a bathroom page? Because planning around therapy and recovery is part of choosing the right fixture. If a hip or knee replacement is coming, getting the bathroom ready before surgery, and knowing how you will get to therapy afterward, makes the recovery period much easier.

Littleton homes and what they mean for a walk-in tub

Older homes near downtown

Character homes can have small bathrooms, narrow doors, and plumbing that has been updated in pieces over the years. We check the route in and plan for surprises behind the wall.

Established ranch and bi-level neighborhoods

Often a standard tub alcove that suits a walk-in tub well. Bi-levels mean a few stairs right inside the front door, which we plan the delivery around.

Newer subdivisions to the south and west

Larger primary baths, frequently upstairs, with a separate shower. A walk-in or roll-in shower conversion is common here.

Staying active: South Suburban programs

South Suburban Parks and Recreation serves much of Littleton, Centennial and nearby communities, and its Buck Recreation Center in Littleton is a hub for older-adult fitness, including strength and balance work. Balance training is one of the things the CDC encourages older adults to do to lower fall risk, alongside medication reviews and home safety changes. A safer bathroom and a balance class make a good pair. Check South Suburban's current active adult schedule for times and fees.

Preparing a Littleton project

  1. Confirm your jurisdiction. City of Littleton, unincorporated county, or a neighboring city. It affects permits and programs.
  2. Measure the bath and the route. Our fit check lists everything.
  3. Decide tub or shower. If a surgery is coming, ask the surgeon what bathing will look like during recovery.
  4. Get an itemized quote. Useful for comparing bids and for any assistance application.
  5. Schedule. Most installs are completed in one day.

Permits: Littleton-area permit rules depend on which city or county your home is in, not on Denver's rules. Your installer handles permits where required.

A pre-surgery bathroom checklist

If a joint replacement or other surgery is on the calendar, the weeks before are the best time to prepare the bathroom. Ask the surgeon's office what bathing will look like during recovery, then:

  • Decide whether a permanent change, such as a walk-in tub or low-threshold shower, should be installed before surgery, and leave a margin of a few weeks.
  • If not, arrange a tub transfer bench or shower chair and a handheld shower for the recovery period.
  • Install properly anchored grab bars by the toilet and the tub or shower.
  • Raise the toilet seat if the surgeon or therapist recommends it.
  • Clear a wide path from the bed to the bathroom for a walker.
  • Line up transportation to therapy, such as Omnibus if you live in the city.

Littleton questions

How do I know if I live inside the City of Littleton?

Your property tax statement or the county assessor's website shows your taxing jurisdictions. A Littleton mailing address alone does not mean you are in the city.

Should I install before or after a joint replacement?

Many people install before, so the bathroom is ready for recovery. Ask your surgeon about bathing restrictions and leave a few weeks of margin.

Do you install in the unincorporated areas around Littleton?

Yes. We install across the Littleton area regardless of which city or county the home is in.

Who do I call for aging services if I live in Littleton?

DRCOG’s Area Agency on Aging serves Arapahoe, Jefferson and Douglas counties, so 303-480-6700 works for any Littleton address. County benefits go through the county your home is in.
