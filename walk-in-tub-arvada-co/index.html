After a fall or a near-miss

If someone in your home has slipped getting out of the tub, or has caught themselves on the towel bar, you are probably reading this page with some urgency. Three things are worth doing right away, before any remodeling:

  1. Tell their doctor. The CDC recommends that older adults talk with a health care provider about any fall and ask for a fall risk check, including a review of medicines.
  2. Make the current bathroom safer tonight. A non-slip mat inside and outside the tub, a night light, and a sturdy shower chair are inexpensive, temporary steps. Do not rely on suction-cup handles or towel bars for support.
  3. Then plan the real fix. If stepping over the tub wall is the problem, a walk-in tub, a low-threshold shower or a roll-in shower removes it.

Apex Park and Recreation District for older adults

Most of Arvada is served by the Apex Park and Recreation District, and its Community Recreation Center at 6842 Wadsworth Blvd. is the main home for active adult programming. Apex PRD's calendar has included a fall prevention workshop presented with the Arvada Fire District, a session for adult children supporting aging parents, and a “No Place Like Home” expo focused on senior housing and in-home services. Those are exactly the kinds of events where families compare notes on bathroom changes. Check the Apex events calendar for current dates.

Apex also points older adults who need help with housing, meals, transportation and similar needs to the Seniors' Resource Center, a long-running Denver-area nonprofit.

Choosing the fixture

Fall history changes the conversation. Here is how we think about it:

Fell stepping over the tub wall

The threshold is the problem. A walk-in tub's low step or a walk-in shower's low curb addresses it directly.

Fell standing in the shower

Balance while standing is the problem. Washing seated, on a tub seat or a shower bench with a handheld shower, matters most.

Fell getting up from the tub floor

A chair-height seat is the answer: either a walk-in tub's built-in seat or a shower bench.

Fell at night on the way

Lighting and the route matter as much as the fixture. Add motion night lights and clear the path.

See our comparison guide for the full trade-offs, and our fall prevention guide for the CDC and NIA recommendations.

Grab bars belong in the plan

Whatever fixture you choose, grab bars are part of a safe bathroom. The National Institute on Aging recommends bars near the toilet and both inside and outside the tub and shower. A walk-in tub comes with bars built in; we also look at where a bar outside the door, and one by the toilet, would help. Grab bars must be anchored properly, into framing or with anchors rated for the purpose.

Arvada sets its own building rules; Denver's do not apply. Moving plumbing or adding a circuit for a jetted tub may need a permit. Your installer handles permits where required.

Quick fixes vs. a full conversion

Not every Arvada bathroom needs a new fixture right away. Here is how the options compare, from least to most change:

ChangeWhat it solvesWhat it does not solve
Non-slip mats and a night lightSlippery surfaces, dark nighttime tripsThe high step over the tub wall
Shower chair or transfer benchStanding to washClimbing in; benches can be awkward in a small tub
Properly anchored grab barsSomething solid to holdThe step itself
Walk-in tubThe step, the low tub floor, and seated soakingWheelchair entry; waiting to fill and drain
Low-threshold or roll-in showerThe step, seated washing, room for a helperNo soak

Many families do the first three now and the fixture change within a few months. That is a sensible order, as long as the temporary steps are not mistaken for the permanent fix. Our cost guide explains what moves the price of a full conversion.

Arvada questions

What can I do this week while we decide on a tub?

Add non-slip mats, a night light, a shower chair and a handheld shower if you can. Have grab bars installed properly. These help immediately and still help after a new fixture goes in.

Is there a fall prevention class in Arvada?

Apex PRD has hosted fall prevention workshops with the Arvada Fire District. Check the Apex events calendar for the next one.
